Who We Are

We are a family-run supply shop that believes the right light and the right hardware make any job easier. Our staff have spent decades in electrical, carpentry and renovation trades, and we only stock what we would install ourselves.

Our mascot, the Honorable Hummingbird, earned the title for tireless service. Hummingbirds visit hundreds of flowers a day without wasting a trip, and that is how we treat your project list: one stop, nothing forgotten.

Why do we do it? Because a well-lit home is safer and calmer, and a well-chosen screw never has to be chosen twice.
